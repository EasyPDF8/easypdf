About EasyPDF

PDF & Study Tools Built for Students, By a Student

Who Runs EasyPDF?

Hi! I'm a student developer who created EasyPDF. This website is my solo project — designed, built, and maintained entirely by me. As a student myself, I understand the challenges of managing documents, studying for exams, and finding reliable free tools online.

I built EasyPDF because I needed these tools for my own studies. Now I'm sharing them with students everywhere — completely free.

Why I Created EasyPDF

As a student, I often found myself in urgent situations — needing to merge PDFs before submission, compress images for assignments, or create flashcards for last-minute exam prep. Most online tools were either paid, had watermarks, or required registration. So I decided to build my own free tools to help students like me.

EasyPDF is here to help students in urgent situations — anytime, anywhere, completely free.

What EasyPDF Helps Students Do

  • PDF Tools: Merge, split, rotate, protect, and edit PDF documents
  • Image Tools: Compress, resize, crop, convert and edit images
  • Flashcard Generator: Create study flashcards from your notes
  • Study Timer: Stay focused with Pomodoro technique
  • Grade Calculator: Calculate your GPA and exam scores
  • Security Tools: Generate strong passwords and check password strength

Security & Privacy Commitment

  • Secure HTTPS Connection: All uploads are encrypted
  • Auto Deletion: Files are automatically deleted after 5 minutes
  • No Permanent Storage: We never store your files permanently
  • No Tracking: No cookies, no analytics tracking your data
  • Privacy First: Your documents stay yours — always

Unlike many other free PDF websites, EasyPDF does not sell your data, show intrusive ads, or require you to create an account. Your privacy matters to me as much as it matters to you.

Why EasyPDF is Completely Free

I believe every student should have access to essential tools without worrying about costs. EasyPDF will always remain free. There are no premium tiers, no hidden fees, and no watermarks. This is my contribution to the student community.

What is Image Border Addition?

Adding a border to an image creates a frame around your photo, making it stand out and look more professional. Our free online tool lets you add custom borders with adjustable size and any color you choose. Perfect for social media posts, product photos, or creative projects.

Common Use Cases

  • Social Media: Make your photos pop with colored borders for posts
  • Product Photography: Add consistent frames to product images for e-commerce
  • Photo Albums: Create uniform borders for photo collections
  • Design Projects: Add frames to images for presentations or portfolios

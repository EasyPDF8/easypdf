What is a PDF Watermark?

A PDF watermark is a semi-transparent text or image overlay placed on a document to indicate ownership, confidentiality, or document status. Common watermarks include "Confidential," "Draft," "Sample," or company names. Our free online tool lets you add custom watermarks to any PDF document with full control over position, size, rotation, color, and opacity.

Why Add Watermarks to PDFs?

  • Protect Intellectual Property: Prevent unauthorized distribution of your documents.
  • Mark Document Status: Clearly label drafts, confidential files, or final versions.
  • Brand Your Documents: Add your company logo or name to all pages.
  • Legal Protection: Watermarks deter copyright infringement.
  • Internal Security: Track document leaks back to their source.

How to Add a Watermark to a PDF

  1. Upload your PDF file by clicking the upload area or dragging and dropping.
  2. Enter your custom watermark text (e.g., "Confidential" or "Draft").
  3. Choose the watermark position: Center, Top Left, Top Right, Bottom Left, or Bottom Right.
  4. Adjust font size (Small, Medium, or Large) to fit your document.
  5. Set rotation angle (0°, 30°, or 45°) for a diagonal watermark effect.
  6. Select a color using the color picker or enter a hex code.
  7. Adjust opacity to make the watermark subtle or prominent.
  8. Preview the result in real-time on the left side.
  9. Click "Add Watermark" to download your watermarked PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions About PDF Watermarking

Is my PDF file uploaded to a server?
No! Your PDF is processed entirely in your browser. We use PDF.js to render and add watermarks locally. Your files never leave your computer, ensuring complete privacy and security.
Can I add a watermark to all pages of a multi-page PDF?
Yes! When you submit your PDF, the watermark is applied to every page of your document. The preview shows the first page, but the final download includes watermarks on all pages.
What file types are supported?
Currently, we support PDF files only. The watermark text can be any custom text you choose. For image watermarks, please check our other tools.
Is there a file size limit?
Since processing happens in your browser, very large PDFs may take longer to process. For optimal performance, we recommend files under 50MB. There is no strict limit imposed by our server.

Why Add Page Numbers to PDF?

Adding page numbers to PDF documents makes them more professional and easier to navigate. Whether you're submitting a report, creating an ebook, or organizing a scanned document, page numbers help readers reference specific sections easily.

Benefits of adding page numbers:

  • Professional appearance: Makes documents look polished and official
  • Easy navigation: Readers can quickly find specific pages
  • Better organization: Especially useful for multi-page reports and presentations
  • Table of contents: Enables accurate page references in tables of contents
  • Citation: Makes citing specific pages easier for academic work
Pro Tip: For documents longer than 10 pages, always add page numbers. Your readers will thank you.

Click the upload area and select the PDF you want to add page numbers to. Files up to 300MB are supported.

Step 3: Choose Position and Format

Select where you want page numbers to appear (bottom center, bottom right, top right, etc.) and choose your format (1, 2, 3 or Page 1, Page 2, etc.).

Step 4: Set Starting Page

Choose which page to start numbering from. Page 1 is default, but you can start from page 2 if the first page is a cover.

Step 5: Add Numbers and Download

Click "Add Page Numbers" and download your PDF with page numbers inserted.

Choosing the Right Position

Different documents work better with different page number positions:

  • Bottom center: Most traditional and common for reports and books
  • Bottom right: Commonly used in business documents
  • Top right: Academic papers often use this position
  • Bottom left: Used for certain document styles
  • Top center: Clean and professional for presentations

Customization Options

Our tool offers several page number formats:

  • Simple numbers: 1, 2, 3, 4
  • With prefix: Page 1, Page 2, Page 3
  • Long format: Page 1 of 10, Page 2 of 10
  • Custom start number: Start from any number (useful for appendices)

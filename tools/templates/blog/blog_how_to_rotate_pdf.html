Why You Might Need to Rotate PDFs

There are several common scenarios where rotating PDF pages becomes necessary:

  • Scanned Documents: When scanning books or documents, pages often come out sideways or upside down
  • Mobile Scans: Photos taken from phones may not align correctly
  • Mixed Orientation: Some PDFs contain both portrait and landscape pages
  • Printing Preparation: Ensure correct orientation before printing
  • Presentation Ready: Fix page orientation for professional presentations
💡 Pro Tip: Always check your PDF orientation before printing. Rotating pages can save paper and ensure your document looks professional!

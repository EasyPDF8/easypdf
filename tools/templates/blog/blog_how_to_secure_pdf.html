Why Password Protect PDFs?

Adding password protection to PDF files is essential for protecting sensitive information. Whether you're sharing financial documents, personal information, or confidential work files, password encryption ensures only authorized people can access your documents.

When you should password protect PDFs:

  • Students: Protect assignments containing personal information
  • Job Seekers: Secure resumes and cover letters with personal contact details
  • Business: Protect contracts, invoices, and employee documents
  • Personal: Secure medical reports, tax documents, or legal papers
Pro Tip: Never send the password in the same email as the protected PDF. Send the password separately via text message, phone call, or a different email.

Step 2: Upload Your PDF

Click the upload area and select the PDF you want to password protect. Files up to 300MB are supported.

Step 3: Set Your Password

Enter a strong password. You'll need this password to open the PDF, so remember it or store it safely.

Step 4: Protect and Download

Click "Protect PDF" and download your encrypted PDF. Anyone trying to open it will need the password you set.

Important: If you forget the password, you cannot recover the PDF. There is no password recovery option. Store your password in a safe place like a password manager.

Tips for Creating Strong Passwords

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Combine uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Include numbers and special characters (!@#$%^&*)
  • Avoid common words, names, or dates (birthdays, "password123")
  • Use a passphrase: "MyDogLoves2Eat!Treats" is stronger than "Dog123"
  • Consider using our Password Generator for strong random passwords

Sharing Password-Protected PDFs

Once your PDF is password protected, share it securely:

  • Email: Attach the PDF in one email, send the password in a separate message
  • Messaging apps: Send the PDF via email, and the password via WhatsApp or text
  • In person: Tell the recipient the password directly

Why Password Protect PDFs?

Adding password protection to PDF files is essential for protecting sensitive information.

Pro Tip: Never send the password in the same email as the protected PDF.

How to Add Password Protection Using EasyPDF

Step 1: Go to the Protect PDF Tool

Visit our Protect PDF tool

Step 2: Upload Your PDF

Select your PDF file.

Step 3: Set Password

Choose a strong password.

Step 4: Download

Click Protect PDF and download.

Important: If you forget the password, recovery may not be possible.

Tips for Creating Strong Passwords

  • Use 12+ characters
  • Use uppercase + lowercase
  • Use numbers + symbols
  • Avoid birthdays or names
  • Use our Password Generator

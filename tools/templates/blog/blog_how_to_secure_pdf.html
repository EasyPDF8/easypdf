April 2026 3 min read Security

Why Protect Your PDFs

  • Confidential Documents: Protect financial or legal papers
  • Personal Information: Secure resumes, IDs, or medical records
  • Business Data: Prevent unauthorized access to company files
  • Client Privacy: Share sensitive information securely
  • Compliance: Meet data protection requirements
💡 Pro Tip: Always use different passwords for different documents. Never use "password" or "123456"!

Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Access the Protect PDF Tool

Go to our Protect PDF tool — no registration required, completely free!

Step 2: Upload Your PDF

Select the PDF file you want to password protect. Files up to 300MB are supported.

Step 3: Enter a Strong Password

Create a secure password (see tips below). Remember it — you'll need it to open the file!

Step 4: Download Your Protected PDF

Click "Protect PDF" and download your encrypted file. Anyone trying to open it will need the password.

⚠️ Important: EasyPDF cannot recover lost passwords. If you forget your password, the file cannot be opened. Save your password safely!

Tips for Strong Passwords

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Mix uppercase and lowercase letters
  • Include numbers and special symbols (!@#$%)
  • Avoid common words or personal information
  • Use a password manager to store your passwords
  • Never share passwords via email or text

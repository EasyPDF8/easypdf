How to Convert Any Website to PDF

April 2026 3 min read Web Tools

Want to save a webpage for offline reading? Converting websites to PDF is the perfect solution. Here's how to do it easily.

📖 What You'll Learn

  • Why Convert Websites to PDF
  • Step-by-Step Guide
  • Pro Tips
  • FAQ

Why Convert Websites to PDF

  • Offline Reading: Access articles without internet
  • Save Receipts: Keep online purchase records
  • Research: Collect web content for projects
  • Share Content: Send webpages as PDF attachments
  • Archive: Preserve web content before it changes
💡 Pro Tip: Use our tool to save articles, receipts, and important web pages before they disappear or change!

Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Copy the URL

Copy the full URL of the webpage you want to save.

Step 2: Use a Website to PDF Tool

While we don't have a direct website-to-PDF tool yet, you can use your browser's "Print to PDF" feature (Ctrl+P → Save as PDF).

Step 3: Save and Organize

Name your PDF file and save it to your computer for easy access.

⚠️ Note: Some websites may block PDF conversion due to copyright or technical restrictions.

Pro Tips

  • Use browser extensions for one-click conversion
  • Remove ads and clutter before saving
  • Choose "Save as PDF" from print dialog (works on any browser)
  • Adjust page margins and scale for better formatting

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I save an entire website as one PDF?

For single pages, use print-to-PDF. For entire sites, you may need specialized tools.

Will images be included?

Yes, most images and formatting are preserved when using print-to-PDF.

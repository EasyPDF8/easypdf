Frequently Asked Questions About Image Compression

Does compression reduce image quality?
Some quality loss may occur at higher compression levels. We offer 4 quality levels (95%, 85%, 75%, 65%) so you can balance file size and quality based on your needs.
What image formats are supported?
We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and BMP formats. These are the most common image formats used for web and photography.
How many images can I compress at once?
You can compress up to 20 images in a single session. The total file size cannot exceed 300MB.
What happens to my uploaded images?
Your images are automatically deleted from our servers within 5 minutes after compression. We never store or access your files permanently.

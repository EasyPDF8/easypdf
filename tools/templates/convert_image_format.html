Frequently Asked Questions About Image Conversion

Does conversion affect image quality?
When converting between formats, we maintain the original quality. Note that converting PNG to JPG will flatten transparency, and JPG to PNG will not restore lost quality from original compression.
What image formats are supported?
We support converting between PNG, JPG/JPEG, WebP, and BMP formats. Input images must be one of these supported formats.
How many images can I convert at once?
You can convert up to 20 images in a single session. The total file size cannot exceed 300MB.

Image Cropper Tool: Trim Unwanted Areas

What is Image Cropping?

Image cropping allows you to remove unwanted outer areas from your images. Our free online tool lets you visually select the area you want to keep and crop multiple images at once. Perfect for removing backgrounds, focusing on subjects, or adjusting composition.

Common Use Cases

  • Social Media: Crop profile pictures and post images to exact dimensions
  • Photography: Improve composition by removing distracting elements
  • E-commerce: Standardize product image dimensions
  • Documents: Remove white space or unwanted areas from scans

How to Crop Images

  1. Upload your images using the button above (up to 20 images)
  2. Click "Crop" on any image to open the visual cropper
  3. Drag and resize the crop box to select the area you want to keep
  4. Click "Apply Crop" to save your changes
  5. Repeat for all images, then click "Save All Cropped Images"

Frequently Asked Questions About Image Cropping

Does cropping reduce image quality?
No, cropping only removes outer areas. The remaining area maintains its original quality. We do not compress or alter the cropped portion.
Can I undo or reset the crop?
Yes! Use the "Reset" button to clear the current crop selection and start over before applying.
How many images can I crop at once?
You can crop up to 20 images in a single session. The total file size cannot exceed 300MB.
What happens to my uploaded images?
Your images are automatically deleted from our servers within 5 minutes after processing. We never store or access your files permanently.

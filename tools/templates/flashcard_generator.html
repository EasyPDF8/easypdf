Flashcard Generator: Create Study Flashcards from Notes

What is a Flashcard Generator?

A flashcard generator creates interactive study cards from your notes. Our free online tool extracts key concepts and definitions to help you memorize information through active recall. Perfect for students preparing for exams, learning new languages, or mastering any subject.

Common Use Cases

  • Students: Create flashcards from lecture notes and textbooks
  • Language Learners: Build vocabulary flashcards
  • Exam Prep: Create study cards for final exams and certifications
  • Self-learners: Master any subject through spaced repetition

How to Use the Flashcard Generator

  1. Paste your study notes (minimum 10 words, up to 5000 characters)
  2. Select number of flashcards (1-15)
  3. Choose difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard)
  4. Click "Generate Flashcards" to create your study deck
  5. Click or tap on flashcards to flip them and see answers
  6. Use navigation buttons to move between cards
  7. Click "Speak" to hear the card content aloud
  8. Use "Shuffle" to randomize card order for better memorization

Frequently Asked Questions About Flashcard Generator

How does the flashcard generator work?
Our tool extracts key concepts from your notes and creates term-definition pairs. You can flip each card to reveal the answer and use audio to hear the content.
What do the difficulty levels mean?
Easy shows basic terms, Medium provides standard definitions, and Hard focuses on explaining concepts in more detail.
How many flashcards can I create?
You can create between 1 and 15 flashcards per session. The number depends on the length and complexity of your notes.
Are my notes stored or shared?
No! Your notes are processed locally in your browser. We never store or access your content. Everything stays private on your device.

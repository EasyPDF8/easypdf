Grade Calculator: Track Your Academic Performance

What is a Grade Calculator?

A grade calculator helps students calculate their weighted average grade, GPA (Grade Point Average), and determine what score they need on a final exam to achieve their desired course grade. Our free online tool supports three calculation modes: weighted grade calculator, GPA calculator on a 4.0 scale, and final exam goal calculator.

Common Use Cases

  • College Students: Calculate semester grades and track GPA across multiple courses.
  • High School Students: Monitor progress in each subject and set grade goals.
  • Teachers & Educators: Demonstrate how weighted grading systems work.
  • Parents: Help children understand their academic standing.
  • Self-learners: Track performance in online courses with weighted assignments.

How to Use the Grade Calculator

  1. Select your calculation mode: Grade Calculator, GPA Calculator, or Final Exam Calculator.
  2. For Grade Calculator: Add assignments with their scores (out of total points) and weight percentages.
  3. For GPA Calculator: Add subjects with letter grades and credit hours (usually 2-4 credits).
  4. For Final Exam Calculator: Enter your current grade, desired grade, and final exam weight.
  5. Click "Calculate" to see your results, letter grade, and personalized feedback.
  6. Use the "Add" buttons to include more assignments or subjects as needed.

Frequently Asked Questions About Grade Calculator

How do weighted grades work?
Weighted grades mean different assignments contribute different percentages to your final grade. For example, if homework is worth 20%, quizzes 30%, and exams 50%, your final grade is calculated by multiplying each category's average by its weight percentage, then adding them together.
What is GPA and how is it calculated?
GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated by converting letter grades to grade points (A=4.0, B=3.0, etc.), multiplying each by the course credits, summing the results, and dividing by total credits. Our calculator uses the standard 4.0 scale accepted by most US colleges.
How accurate is the final exam calculator?
The final exam calculator is mathematically accurate based on the formula: (Desired Grade - (Current Grade × (1 - Weight/100))) ÷ (Weight/100). It tells you exactly what percentage you need on your final to reach your target grade, assuming your current grade is accurate.
Are my calculations saved or shared?
No! All calculations are performed locally in your browser. We never store or share your grade data. Everything stays private on your device. You can close the page and your data will not be retained.

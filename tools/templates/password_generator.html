What is a Password Generator?

A password generator is a security tool that creates random, unpredictable passwords using a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Our free online generator creates cryptographically strong passwords that are resistant to brute-force attacks and dictionary-based hacking attempts.

Why Use a Password Generator?

  • Eliminate Weak Passwords: Avoid common passwords like "password123" or "qwerty".
  • Create Unique Passwords: Generate different passwords for every account you own.
  • Save Time: Stop struggling to think of "creative" passwords.
  • Enhanced Security: Random passwords are exponentially harder to crack.
  • Meet Requirements: Easily create passwords that meet website complexity rules.

Frequently Asked Questions About Password Generator

Are the generated passwords truly random and secure?
Yes! Our password generator uses JavaScript's crypto-safe randomization to create unpredictable passwords. The randomness is suitable for generating secure passwords for online accounts, email, and other sensitive services.
What password length should I choose?
We recommend at least 12-16 characters for good security. For high-value accounts like email or banking, use 20+ characters. Longer passwords are exponentially harder for hackers to crack through brute-force attacks.
Are my generated passwords saved or stored anywhere?
No! All password generation happens locally in your browser. We never store, save, or transmit your generated passwords anywhere. Once you close the page, the password is gone from our end.
Should I use a password manager with this generator?
Absolutely! Password managers like Bitwarden, LastPass, or 1Password can securely store all your generated passwords. This allows you to use unique, complex passwords for every site without having to memorize them.

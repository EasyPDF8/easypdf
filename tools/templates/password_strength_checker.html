What is a Password Strength Checker?

A password strength checker is a security tool that analyzes your password and evaluates how resistant it would be to brute-force attacks or guessing. Our free online tool checks length, character diversity (uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters), common password patterns, and estimates how long it would take a hacker to crack your password.

Why Password Strength Matters

  • Protect Personal Information: Weak passwords are the #1 cause of account breaches.
  • Prevent Unauthorized Access: Strong passwords keep hackers out of your email, banking, and social media.
  • Secure Work Accounts: Many companies require strong passwords for employee security.
  • Avoid Identity Theft: Weak passwords can lead to stolen identities and financial loss.
  • Meet Security Standards: Many websites now enforce strong password requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions About Password Strength

Is my password sent to a server or stored anywhere?
No! Your password is processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. It never leaves your computer, is not stored, and is not sent to any server. Your privacy is completely protected.
What makes a password "very strong"?
A very strong password is at least 12 characters long and contains a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. It should not be a common word, name, or predictable pattern like "password123" or "qwerty".
How is crack time calculated?
Crack time is estimated based on password length and character set size. The calculation assumes a hacker can try 1 billion combinations per second. Longer passwords with more character types take exponentially longer to crack.
Should I use a password manager?
Yes! Password managers generate and store extremely strong, unique passwords for all your accounts. They're the best way to maintain strong security without having to remember dozens of complex passwords.

1. Information We Collect

EasyPDF is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not permanently store your files. All uploaded files are automatically deleted after 5 minutes. We collect minimal information:

  • Usage data - File sizes and number of files processed (for rate limiting)
  • Session information - Temporary session data to track your uploads
  • IP addresses - Anonymized for rate limiting and abuse prevention

No account? No problem! You can use all tools without creating an account. We don't collect names, email addresses, or any personal identification.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve our free PDF and image tools
  • Enforce usage limits (300MB per file, 100 files per day)
  • Prevent abuse and ensure fair usage for all users
  • Monitor server performance and fix issues

3. File Processing & Storage

We DO NOT store your files. Here's what happens:

  • Files are uploaded to our temporary server for processing
  • Processing happens immediately
  • Files are automatically deleted within 5 minutes
  • We never access, view, or share your file contents
  • No permanent copy of your files is kept anywhere

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies only for:

  • Maintaining your session (tracking your uploaded files)
  • Enforcing daily usage limits (100 files per day)
  • Preventing abuse of our free service

These cookies are necessary for the website to function. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work properly.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may collect data using cookies to show relevant ads. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • Files are processed in isolated temporary storage
  • Automatic cleanup every 5 minutes
  • No permanent databases of your files

7. Your Rights

Since we don't store your files or collect personal information, there's nothing to delete. However, you have the right to:

  • Stop using our service at any time
  • Clear your browser cookies to reset session data
  • Contact us with privacy concerns

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is available to all users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The latest version will always be posted on this page with the effective date.

Frequently Asked Questions About PDF Protection

What encryption does your tool use?
We use AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) encryption, which is the industry standard for PDF security. This ensures your files are protected with military-grade encryption.
Can I remove password protection later?
Yes! Use our Remove Password tool to remove password protection from your PDF files (you will need the password to unlock it first).
What happens if I forget my password?
We cannot recover lost passwords. Please save your password in a secure place. Without the password, encrypted PDFs cannot be opened.
Is there a page limit for password protection?
Yes, the PDF cannot exceed 100 pages. This ensures fast processing and stable performance for all users.
What makes a strong password?
A strong password should be at least 12 characters long, include uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. Avoid common words or personal information.

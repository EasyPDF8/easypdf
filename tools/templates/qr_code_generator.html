What is a QR Code Generator?

A QR code generator creates Quick Response codes that can be scanned by smartphones to instantly access information. Our free online tool lets you create custom QR codes for URLs, text, email addresses, phone numbers, WiFi networks, and vCard contact information. Customize colors and download as PNG.

Common Use Cases

  • Business: Create QR codes for websites, menus, and product information
  • Marketing: Generate QR codes for promotions, coupons, and landing pages
  • Personal: Share contact information, WiFi credentials, or event details
  • Education: Create QR codes for study materials and resource links

How to Generate QR Codes

  1. Enter your text, URL, or other information (up to 500 characters)
  2. Customize foreground and background colors (optional)
  3. Click "Generate QR Code" to create your QR code
  4. Download your QR code as PNG image

Frequently Asked Questions About QR Codes

What can I encode in a QR code?
You can encode URLs, plain text, email addresses, phone numbers, WiFi credentials, vCard contact information, SMS messages, and more.
Can I customize QR code colors?
Yes! You can choose any foreground and background colors using our color picker. Dark foreground on light background works best for scanning.
Is there a character limit?
Yes, the content cannot exceed 500 characters. This ensures optimal QR code size and scannability.
How do I scan a QR code?
Use your smartphone's camera. Most modern phones have built-in QR scanning. Just open your camera app and point it at the QR code.

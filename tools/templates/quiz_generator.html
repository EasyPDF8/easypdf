What is a Quiz Generator?

A quiz generator creates multiple-choice questions automatically from your study notes. Our free online tool extracts key concepts and generates practice quizzes to help you test your knowledge. Perfect for exam preparation, self-assessment, and active recall practice.

Common Use Cases

  • Students: Create practice quizzes from lecture notes and textbooks
  • Teachers: Generate quick assessments from lesson materials
  • Self-learners: Test understanding of online courses and tutorials
  • Exam Prep: Create custom practice tests for final exams

How to Use the Quiz Generator

  1. Paste your study notes (minimum 50 characters, up to 5000 characters)
  2. Select number of questions (1-20)
  3. Click "Generate Quiz" to create your practice test
  4. Answer the multiple-choice questions
  5. Submit to see your score and correct answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Quiz Generator

How does the quiz generator work?
Our tool extracts key concepts from your notes using keyword analysis. It then creates multiple-choice questions including fill-in-the-blank, true/false, and definition-based questions.
What types of questions are generated?
The generator creates various question types including fill-in-the-blank, definition matching, true/false statements, and concept association questions.
How many questions can I generate?
You can generate between 1 and 20 questions per quiz. The number depends on the length and complexity of your notes.
Are my notes stored or shared?
No! Your notes are processed locally in your browser. We never store or access your content. Everything stays private on your device.

What is PDF Password Removal?

PDF password removal is the process of decrypting a password-protected PDF file to create an unlocked version that anyone can open, view, and edit. Our free online tool helps you remove both user passwords (required to open the document) and owner passwords (that restrict editing, printing, or copying).

Frequently Asked Questions About PDF Password Removal

Is it legal to remove passwords from PDF files?
Yes, it is legal to remove passwords from PDF files that you own or have permission to modify. Do not use this tool to bypass security on documents you don't own or don't have authorization to access.
Will this tool crack or guess my password?
No. This tool requires you to enter the correct password. It does not crack, guess, or recover lost passwords. You must know the password to unlock the PDF.
What's the difference between user and owner passwords?
A user password (or document open password) is required to open and view the PDF. An owner password (or permissions password) restricts actions like printing, editing, or copying. Our tool removes both types of protection.
Are my files kept private and secure?
Yes. Your files are processed securely and automatically deleted from our servers within 5 minutes of processing. We do not store or share your documents or passwords.

What is Image Resizing?

Image resizing allows you to change the dimensions of your images while maintaining aspect ratio. Our free online tool lets you resize multiple images at once. Perfect for social media, websites, email attachments, or printing.

Common Use Cases

  • Social Media: Resize images for profile pictures, posts, and covers
  • Website Owners: Optimize image dimensions for faster loading
  • Students: Resize images for assignments and presentations
  • Email: Reduce image dimensions for smaller attachments

How to Resize Images

  1. Upload your images using the button above (up to 20 images)
  2. Click "Resize" on any image to open the settings panel
  3. Enter desired width or height (leave one empty to maintain aspect ratio)
  4. Click "Apply to Current Image" to save your changes
  5. Repeat for all images, then click "Save All Resized Images"

Frequently Asked Questions About Image Resizing

Will resizing affect image quality?
Enlarging images may cause quality loss. Reducing size generally preserves quality. We recommend not enlarging beyond original dimensions.
How does the aspect ratio work?
When you enter width only, height is automatically calculated to maintain proportions. Enter height only for the same effect.
How many images can I resize at once?
You can resize up to 20 images in a single session. The total file size cannot exceed 300MB.
What happens to my uploaded images?
Your images are automatically deleted from our servers within 5 minutes after processing. We never store or access your files permanently.

What is Image Rotation?

Image rotation allows you to change the orientation of your images by 90°, 180°, or 270° clockwise. Our free online tool lets you rotate multiple images at once with live preview. Perfect for fixing sideways photos or adjusting image orientation.

Common Use Cases

  • Photography: Fix incorrectly oriented photos from cameras or phones
  • Scanned Documents: Correct orientation of scanned images
  • Social Media: Adjust image orientation before posting
  • Presentations: Standardize image orientation for slides

How to Rotate Images

  1. Upload your images using the button above (up to 20 images)
  2. Select your desired rotation angle (90°, 180°, or 270°)
  3. Click "Preview" on any image to see the rotation effect
  4. Click "Rotate Images" to apply the same rotation to all images

Frequently Asked Questions About Image Rotation

Does rotating affect image quality?
No, rotation does not affect image quality. Your image remains exactly the same quality, just with corrected orientation.
Can I rotate images in different directions?
Yes! We support 90° clockwise, 180°, and 270° clockwise (90° counter-clockwise). These cover all common rotation needs.
How many images can I rotate at once?
You can rotate up to 20 images in a single session. The total file size cannot exceed 300MB.
What happens to my uploaded images?
Your images are automatically deleted from our servers within 5 minutes after processing. We never store or access your files permanently.

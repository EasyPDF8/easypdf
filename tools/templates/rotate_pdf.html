PDF Rotator Tool: Fix Page Orientation

What is a PDF Rotator?

A PDF rotator lets you change the orientation of pages in a PDF document. Our free online tool can rotate pages 90°, 180°, or 270° permanently. Perfect for fixing scanned documents that are upside down or sideways.

Common Use Cases

  • Scanned Documents: Fix pages that were scanned upside down or sideways
  • Mobile Scans: Correct orientation of photos taken from phones
  • Mixed Orientation: Standardize portrait and landscape pages in one PDF
  • Print Preparation: Ensure correct page orientation before printing

How to Rotate PDF Pages

  1. Upload your PDF file using the button above
  2. Select your desired rotation angle (90°, 180°, or 270°)
  3. Click the Rotate PDF button

Frequently Asked Questions About Rotating PDFs

Does rotating a PDF reduce image quality?
No. Rotating PDF pages does not affect image quality or resolution. Your document remains exactly the same quality, just with corrected orientation.
Can I rotate only specific pages?
Our tool rotates all pages in the PDF. To rotate specific pages, first use our Split PDF tool to extract pages, rotate them separately, then merge back using Merge PDF.
Is the rotation permanent?
Yes. The rotation is permanently applied to the PDF file. You can always upload the rotated file again to rotate it further if needed.
What rotation angles are supported?
We support 90° clockwise, 180° (upside down), and 270° clockwise (90° counter-clockwise). These cover all common orientation fixes.
Is there a page limit for rotating?
Yes, the PDF cannot exceed 100 pages. This ensures fast processing and stable performance for all users.
What happens if my PDF has more than 100 pages?
The file will be rejected with a clear error message. Please use our Split PDF tool to split it into smaller parts first.

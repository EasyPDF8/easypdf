What is a Study Timetable Generator?

A study timetable generator helps you create a personalized study schedule based on your subjects, available hours, and study days. Our free online tool creates a balanced schedule that rotates subjects evenly, ensuring you cover all topics effectively. Perfect for exam preparation, semester planning, and daily study routines.

Common Use Cases

  • Exam Preparation: Create a structured plan for final exams
  • Students: Organize daily study routines for multiple subjects
  • Working Professionals: Plan study time for certifications
  • Parents: Create study schedules for children

How to Use the Study Timetable Generator

  1. Add your subjects (up to 20 subjects)
  2. Set study hours per day (1-12 hours)
  3. Choose number of days for your plan (1-30 days)
  4. Select your preferred start time
  5. Click "Generate Timetable" to create your schedule
  6. Download as PDF for offline reference

Frequently Asked Questions About Study Timetable

How are subjects distributed in the timetable?
Subjects are rotated evenly across study days. Each subject gets equal study time, and the rotation ensures variety and prevents boredom.
Can I customize the start time?
Yes! You can choose any custom start time. The timetable will schedule study sessions based on your preferred starting hour.
How many subjects can I add?
You can add up to 20 subjects. Each subject will receive equal study time in the rotation.
Can I download my timetable?
Yes! After generating your timetable, click the "Download PDF" button to save your schedule for offline use and printing.

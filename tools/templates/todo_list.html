To-Do List & Task Manager: Stay Productive

What is a To-Do List?

A to-do list helps you organize tasks, prioritize work, and track progress. Our free online task manager lets you create tasks with priority levels (High, Medium, Low), mark them as complete, edit or delete tasks, and save everything to the cloud. Perfect for students, professionals, and anyone who wants to stay organized.

Common Use Cases

  • Students: Track assignments, homework, and exam preparation tasks
  • Professionals: Manage work projects, deadlines, and daily tasks
  • Personal: Organize shopping lists, chores, and personal goals
  • Study Groups: Share and track group project tasks

How to Use the To-Do List

  1. Enter your task in the input field (up to 200 characters)
  2. Select priority level: High (red), Medium (orange), or Low (green)
  3. Click "Add" to create your task
  4. Check the box to mark tasks as complete
  5. Use filters to view All, Active, or Completed tasks
  6. Click "Save to Cloud" to store your tasks

Frequently Asked Questions About To-Do List

How do I save my tasks?
Tasks are automatically saved to your browser's storage. Click "Save to Cloud" to store them permanently and access from any device.
What do the priority colors mean?
Red = High priority (urgent tasks), Orange = Medium priority (important but not urgent), Green = Low priority (can be done later).
Can I edit or delete tasks?
Yes! Click the edit (pencil) icon to modify a task, or the trash icon to delete it. You can also delete all completed tasks at once.
Is there a limit on how many tasks I can create?
You can create up to 500 tasks. This ensures smooth performance and a clutter-free experience.

Word Counter: Analyze Your Text Online

What is a Word Counter?

A word counter helps you analyze text by counting words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimating reading time. Our free online tool supports typing directly, pasting text, or uploading TXT and PDF files. Perfect for students, writers, content creators, and professionals.

Common Use Cases

  • Students: Check essay and assignment word counts
  • Writers: Track article and blog post length
  • Content Creators: Measure social media caption length
  • Professionals: Analyze reports and document length

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text directly into the text area (up to 100,000 characters)
  2. Upload a TXT or PDF file for automatic text extraction
  3. View real-time statistics including words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs
  4. Check reading time and speaking time estimates
  5. See top keywords to understand content focus
  6. Copy text or clear with one click

Frequently Asked Questions About Word Counter

Can I upload PDF files?
Yes! You can upload both TXT and PDF files. We extract text from PDFs automatically for accurate counting.
How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is based on average reading speed of 200 words per minute. Speaking time uses 150 words per minute.
What are the character limits?
The text area supports up to 100,000 characters. This is sufficient for most documents, essays, and articles.
Is my text stored or shared?
No! Your text is processed locally in your browser. We never store or access your content. Everything stays private.
